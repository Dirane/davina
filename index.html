Soothe

Calming sounds to help your baby settle.

Safe listening

  • Keep the phone at least 2 metres (about 6 feet) from your baby.
  • Keep the volume low. It is limited to 70% here, but your phone's own volume matters too.
  • Use the sleep timer rather than playing sound all night.

    Phone 2 m away, low volume, and the sleep timer on. Sounds work offline.
